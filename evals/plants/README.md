# Plant Gene Set Benchmark: Evaluating GOA Evidence Variants Across *Arabidopsis* and Five DOE/BER Bioenergy & Oilseed Crops

This evaluation scores four Gene Ontology Annotation (GOA) evidence-code slices (`all`, `no_contributes_to`, `iba_iea`, `iba`) against the **30-set, 6-species plant GO-interpretation gold standard** in [`curation/plants/genesets/`](../../curation/plants/README.md). Together with the human benchmark in [`evals/iba_vs_benchmark/`](../iba_vs_benchmark/README.md), it tests how ontology structure, experimental literature curation, phylogenetic `IBA` projection, and automated domain-rule `IEA` interact across model and non-model plant genomes.

---

## 1. The Three Annotation Tiers of Plant Functional Genomics

In human functional genomics (`NCBITaxon:9606`), every query set is enriched against a single deeply curated species GAF (`goa_human.gaf.gz`). In plant biology — and particularly across **DOE Biological and Environmental Research (BER)** bioenergy feedstocks and oilseed crops — official GOA proteomes stratify into **three structurally distinct annotation regimes**:

| Species | Taxon | Common Name / Role | Annotation Tier | Annotated Genes | Total Annotations | Experimental (`EXP`/`IDA`/`IMP`/`IGI`/`IEP`/`HDA`) | Phylogenetic (`IBA`) | Automated (`IEA`) | Other (`IC`/`TAS`/`ISS`/`RCA`) |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ***Arabidopsis thaliana*** | `NCBITaxon:3702` | Thale cress (primary dicot model) | **Tier 1: Deep Experimental + IBA + IEA** | 29,290 | 269,785 | **104,033 (38.6%)** | 59,003 (21.9%) | 79,572 (29.5%) | 27,177 (10.1%) |
| ***Populus trichocarpa*** | `NCBITaxon:3694` | Black cottonwood (woody bioenergy tree) | **Tier 2: PANTHER IBA + Automated IEA** | 30,278 | 217,328 | 19 (0.01%) | **66,019 (30.4%)** | 151,290 (69.6%) | 0 (0.0%) |
| ***Sorghum bicolor*** | `NCBITaxon:4558` | Sorghum (C4 bioenergy & drought-tolerant grass) | **Tier 2: PANTHER IBA + Automated IEA** | 25,640 | 175,931 | 3 (0.00%) | **50,418 (28.7%)** | 125,497 (71.3%) | 13 (0.0%) |
| ***Brachypodium distachyon*** | `NCBITaxon:15368` | Purple false brome (DOE grass/AM symbiosis model) | **Tier 2: PANTHER IBA + Automated IEA** | 21,798 | 208,286 | 29 (0.01%) | **45,816 (22.0%)** | 162,438 (78.0%) | 3 (0.0%) |
| ***Panicum virgatum*** | `NCBITaxon:38727` | Switchgrass (flagship perennial C4 bioenergy grass) | **Tier 3: Automated IEA-Only** | 52,589 | 215,075 | 0 (0.0%) | **0 (0.0%)** | **215,075 (100.0%)** | 0 (0.0%) |
| ***Thlaspi arvense*** | `NCBITaxon:13288` | Field pennycress (winter oilseed / aviation biofuel crop) | **Tier 3: Automated IEA-Only** | 20,762 | 124,043 | 0 (0.0%) | **0 (0.0%)** | **124,043 (100.0%)** | 0 (0.0%) |

This three-tier gradient makes the 30-set plant benchmark a natural stress test for homology and rule-based functional annotation:
- **Tier 1 (*Arabidopsis*, 14 sets)** measures what is lost when decades of experimental TAIR mutant/biochemical curation (`IMP`/`IDA`/`IGI`) are stripped down to `iba_iea` or `iba`, and conversely how much experimental pleiotropy and high-throughput proteomics (`HDA`) noise those literature annotations introduce.
- **Tier 2 (*Populus*, *Sorghum*, *Brachypodium*, 9 sets)** represents established DOE/JGI reference crops included in the PANTHER phylogenetic tree build (`~22–30%` `IBA` + `~70–78%` `IEA`, `<0.02%` experimental), testing whether restricting to `iba` improves precision over `iba_iea` when no direct experimental GAF curation exists.
- **Tier 3 (*Panicum virgatum*, *Thlaspi arvense*, 7 sets)** represents emerging polyploid and domesticated DOE bioenergy crops not yet integrated into PANTHER (`0%` `IBA`, `100%` InterPro2GO / UniRule / UniProt-Keyword `IEA`), exposing both the surprising power and the systematic blind spots of purely automated domain/rule projection.

## 3. Stratified Performance Across the Three Plant Annotation Tiers

Pooling all 30 sets masks dramatic, opposite trade-offs across the three annotation tiers:

### Tier 1: *Arabidopsis thaliana* (14 sets — Experimental + PANTHER IBA + IEA)

| Variant | Sets | Core Recall (`80` terms) | Confirmatory (`60` terms) | Mechanistic (`52` terms) | Judged Precision | Judged F1 | False Positives Fired (`28` traps) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `all` | 14 | **0.925** | **0.933** | **0.962** | 0.855 | **0.898** | 18 / 28 |
| `no_contributes_to` | 14 | **0.925** | **0.933** | **0.962** | 0.855 | **0.898** | 18 / 28 |
| `iba_iea` | 14 | 0.575 | 0.700 | 0.442 | 0.929 | 0.714 | 5 / 28 |
| `iba` | 14 | 0.362 | 0.450 | 0.288 | **0.933** | 0.535 | **3 / 28** |

1. **Mechanistic recall drops more than 2× when experimental TAIR annotations are removed (`0.962` $\to$ `0.442` in `iba_iea`, `0.288` in `iba`)**:
   - Mirroring the human benchmark finding that mechanistic insight is ~2× harder to recover without experimental annotations, angiosperm-specific developmental, physiological, and immune mechanisms in *Arabidopsis* are almost exclusively supported by mutant genetics (`IMP`/`IGI`) and direct assays (`IDA`).
   - Stripping experimental codes loses root hair tip growth (`GO:0048768`) and polarized elongation (`GO:0048767`), Casparian strip assembly (`GO:0048226`) and suberin biosynthesis (`GO:0010345`), stomatal blue-light opening (`GO:1902456`) and closure (`GO:0090333`), pattern-recognition receptor signaling (`GO:0002221`), camalexin (`GO:0010120`) and pipecolic acid (`GO:0062034`) biosynthesis, Strategy I coumarin secretion (`GO:0009804`), and PHR1 galactolipid remodeling (`GO:0019375`).
2. **Conversely, `iba_iea` eliminates 72% of false positives in *Arabidopsis* (`18/28` $\to$ `5/28`, raising precision from `0.855` to `0.929`)**:
   - Removing experimental (`IMP`/`IGI`/`IDA`/`HDA`) annotations strips out whole-leaf mRNA-binding proteomics artifacts (`GO:0003729` in `ARATH_LEAF_MESOPHYLL`), developmental organ-reuse pleiotropy (`trichome branching` `GO:0010091` in `ARATH_ROOT_ATRICHOBLAST`, `inflorescence development` `GO:0010229` in `ARATH_PTI_FLG22_IMMUNITY`, `maintenance of root meristem identity` `GO:0010078` in `ARATH_PHLOEM_SIEVE_ELEMENT_COMPANION`), and stress-hormone cross-induction (`response to abscisic acid` `GO:0009737` in `ARATH_CBF_COLD_ACCLIMATION`).
   - The **only** false positives that survive in `iba` (`3/28`) are structural GO–ChEBI chemical-hierarchy roll-ups (`response to alcohol` `GO:0097305` and `response to lipid` `GO:0033993` on `ARATH_ABA_DROUGHT_RESPONSE`, and `tricarboxylic acid biosynthetic process` `GO:0072351` on nicotianamine synthases in `ARATH_IRON_DEFICIENCY_FIT_COUMARIN`).

| Variant | Sets | Core Recall (`51` terms) | Confirmatory (`42` terms) | Mechanistic (`35` terms) | Judged Precision | Judged F1 | False Positives Fired (`27` traps) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `all` | 9 | **0.843** | **0.881** | **0.914** | 0.742 | **0.812** | 24 / 27 |
| `no_contributes_to` | 9 | **0.843** | **0.881** | **0.914** | 0.742 | **0.812** | 24 / 27 |

1. **`all`, `no_contributes_to`, and `iba_iea` are identical**: Because EBI GOA for poplar, sorghum, and *Brachypodium* contains `<0.02%` experimental annotations, filtering out experimental codes changes nothing.
2. **Restricting from `iba_iea` to `iba` cuts false positives nearly in half (`24/27` $\to$ `13/27`), raising precision from `0.742` to `0.780`**:
   - PANTHER `iba` eliminates 11 major domain/rule `IEA` false positives across the 3 species, including white-rot fungal `lignin catabolic process` (`GO:0046274`) and `phenylpropanoid catabolic process` (`GO:0046271`) on polymerizing plant laccases in *Populus* and *Sorghum*, bacterial/animal `histidine ammonia-lyase activity` (`GO:0004397`) on plant `PAL` genes, *Xenopus*-oocyte `salicin transport` (`GO:0042948`) and `maltose:proton symporter activity` (`GO:0005364`) on sorghum `SbSUT` sucrose transporters, and *Arabidopsis* leaf `trichome morphogenesis` (`GO:0010090`) on poplar spring bud-flush C2H2 transcription factors.
   - However, `iba` retains 13 false positives driven either by **GO–ChEBI hormone roll-ups** (`GO:0097305`, `GO:0033993`) or by **PANTHER family-wide over-projection** across large plant paralog families (`plant-type primary cell wall biogenesis` `GO:0009833` and `mitotic cytokinesis` `GO:0000281` on secondary-wall CESAs in *Populus* and *Brachypodium*, and `long-chain fatty acid-CoA ligase activity` `GO:0004467` on 4-coumarate-CoA ligase `Bmr2` in *Sorghum*).

- **Zero PANTHER `IBA` coverage (`0.000` recall in `iba`)**: Neither switchgrass (`NCBITaxon:38727`) nor field pennycress (`NCBITaxon:13288`) is included in current PANTHER phylogenetic tree projections. Any pipeline that filters plant GAFs to `IBA` (or `EXP + IBA`) completely blanks out these emerging DOE bioenergy crops.
- **High biochemical enzyme recovery (`0.786` core recall) paired with systematic higher-order physiological `annotation_gap`s**: InterPro2GO and UniRule `IEA` recover `78.6%` of core terms because core metabolic enzymes (`PEPC`, `PPDK`, `NAD-ME`, `RuBisCO`, `FAE1`, `DGAT1`, `CYP79B2`, `CYP83B1`, `SOT16`, `GS1`, `SGR`) carry conserved catalytic domains. Yet higher-order physiological and anatomical terms (`C4 photosynthesis` `GO:0009760`, `NAD-malic enzyme C4 photosynthesis` `GO:0009763`, `very long-chain fatty acid biosynthetic process` `GO:0042761`, `seed oilbody biogenesis` `GO:0010344`, `response to herbivore` `GO:0080027`) have **0 annotations** in their GAFs (`annotation_gap`).

## 5. Taxonomy of Nine Plant-Specific GO Enrichment Failure Modes

Evaluating all 30 sets across the 4 evidence variants reveals **nine systematic failure modes** in plant GO enrichment, spanning ontology hierarchy design (`go-basic.obo`), cross-kingdom `IEA` domain rules, PANTHER `IBA` paralog projection, herbaceous model bias, and high-throughput experimental noise:

### 5.1. GO–ChEBI Chemical-Ontology Hierarchy Roll-Up Artifacts (`all`, `no_contributes_to`, `iba_iea`, `iba`)

### 5.2. Vertebrate-Centric GO Anatomical/System Roll-Up Artifacts (`all`, `iba_iea`)

Plant long-distance vascular transport (`xylem` and `phloem`) shares high-level GO parent terms with vertebrate cardiovascular physiology:

### 5.3. Cross-Kingdom Fungal-to-Plant `IEA` Enzymatic Reversal (Eliminated by `iba`)

One of the most striking biological inversions in the benchmark occurs in woody and grass lignification modules:
- In [`POPTR_SECONDARY_XYLEM_LIGNIN`](../../curation/plants/genesets/POPTR_SECONDARY_XYLEM_LIGNIN.yaml) ($8/35$ genes, $p_{\text{adj}} = 5.76 \times 10^{-11}$) and [`SORBI_BM_MONOLIGNOL_CELL_WALL`](../../curation/plants/genesets/SORBI_BM_MONOLIGNOL_CELL_WALL.yaml) ($4/40$ genes, $p_{\text{adj}} = 1.71 \times 10^{-3}$), apoplastic plant laccases (`PtLAC`/`SbLAC` multicopper oxidases) that **oxidatively polymerize** monolignols into secondary cell wall lignin are enriched for **`GO:0046274` (`lignin catabolic process`)** and **`GO:0046271` (`phenylpropanoid catabolic process`)** in `all`, `no_contributes_to`, and `iba_iea`.
- **Root cause**: UniRule/InterPro2GO automated `IEA` rules map the conserved cupredoxin multicopper oxidase domain (`IPR001117` / `KW-0432`) to lignin degradation (`GO:0046274`), which is the biological function of extracellular laccases in **white-rot basidiomycete fungi** (*Trametes versicolor*, *Phanerochaete chrysosporium*) that depolymerize wood, the exact opposite of vascular plant xylem laccases.
- **Resolution by `iba`**: Because PANTHER phylogenetic trees separate plant clade laccases from fungal basidiomycete laccases, restricting to `iba` **completely eliminates** `GO:0046274` and `GO:0046271` in both *Populus* and *Sorghum*.

### 5.7. Herbaceous/Temperate/Non-Mycorrhizal *Arabidopsis* Model Bias Projected onto Crops

Because *Arabidopsis thaliana* is an **annual**, **temperate**, **herbaceous dicot** that **evolutionarily lost the common symbiotic signaling pathway**, relying on *Arabidopsis*-centric homology and UniRule projection creates severe **annotation gaps** (`23` core gaps across the benchmark) and **ecological/anatomical false projections** in perennial, tropical, monocot, and mycorrhizal bioenergy crops:

1. **The *Arabidopsis* Non-Mycorrhizal Evolutionary Blind Spot (`BRADI_ARBUSCULAR_MYCORRHIZAL_SYMBIOSIS`)**:
   - Over 80% of land plants — including DOE bioenergy grasses (*Brachypodium*, *Panicum*, *Sorghum*) and woody trees (*Populus*) — form arbuscular mycorrhizal (AM) endosymbioses with Glomeromycotina fungi via a conserved toolkit (`PT4`, `STR1`/`STR2`, `RAM1`, `RAM2`, `RAD1`, `SYMRK`/`DMI2`, `CCaMK`/`DMI3`, `CYCLOPS`/`IPD3`, `VPY`, `PAM1`).
   - Because Brassicaceae (*Arabidopsis*) lost the entire AM-specific gene cohort (`PT4`, `STR1/2`, `RAM1`, `CCaMK`, `CYCLOPS`, `RAD1`), *Arabidopsis*-anchored GO projection leaves **0 genes** in the *Brachypodium* GOA proteome annotated to **`GO:0036377` (`arbuscular mycorrhizal association`, `annotation_gap`)** or **`GO:0085042` (`periarbuscular membrane`, `annotation_gap`)**.
2. **Dicot Type I vs. Commelinid Grass Type II Cell Wall Feruloylation (`BRADI_GRASS_TYPE_II_CELL_WALL_FERULATE`)**:
   - Grass cell walls cross-link glucuronoarabinoxylan (GAX) to lignin via ferulate and *p*-coumarate esters synthesized by BAHD acyltransferases (`BdPMT1`, `BdAT10`, `BdAT1`–`BdAT9`), the primary recalcitrance barrier in cellulosic biofuels. Because dicots (*Arabidopsis*) lack feruloylated GAX, **0 genes** in *Brachypodium* are annotated to **`GO:0033495` (`ferulate biosynthetic process`, `annotation_gap`)**.
3. **Annual Herbaceous vs. Perennial Woody Seasonal Dormancy (`POPTR_AUTUMN_BUD_DORMANCY_ABA`, `POPTR_SPRING_BUD_FLUSH_CHILLING`)**:
   - Perennial trees (*Populus*) cycle through autumn photoperiodic bud endodormancy (plasmodesmatal callose sphincters) and winter chilling-induced spring bud flush. In `goa_populus_trichocarpa.gaf.gz`, **0 genes** are annotated to **`GO:0097207` (`bud dormancy process`, `annotation_gap`)** or **`GO:0097438` (`exit from dormancy`, `annotation_gap`)**.
   - Worse, in [`POPTR_SPRING_BUD_FLUSH_CHILLING`](../../curation/plants/genesets/POPTR_SPRING_BUD_FLUSH_CHILLING.yaml), 6 poplar C2H2 zinc-finger transcription factorsorthologous to *Arabidopsis* `GIS`/`ZFP5`/`ZFP8` trigger **`GO:0010090` (`trichome morphogenesis`, $6/34$ genes, $p_{\text{adj}} = 3.11 \times 10^{-9}$ in `all`/`iba_iea`)** because in *Arabidopsis* those GA-inducible C2H2 TFs were characterized as regulators of epidermal leaf trichome initiation rather than perennial woody bud break.
4. **Temperate Freezing vs. Tropical C4 Drought Dehydrins (`SORBI_STAY_GREEN_DROUGHT_TOLERANCE`)**:
   - In tropical C4 sorghum [`SORBI_STAY_GREEN_DROUGHT_TOLERANCE`](../../curation/plants/genesets/SORBI_STAY_GREEN_DROUGHT_TOLERANCE.yaml), post-flowering drought dehydrins (`LEA`/rab16B/COR47 homologs) and ABA-responsive phosphatases trigger **`GO:0009409` (`response to cold`, $10/33$ genes, $p_{\text{adj}} = 3.15 \times 10^{-12}$ in `all`/`iba_iea`; $6/33$, $p_{\text{adj}} = 6.93 \times 10^{-8}$ in `iba`)** because their *Arabidopsis* orthologs (`COR47`, `ERD10`, `RAB18`) are annotated to cold acclimation.
5. **Single-Mutant Pleiotropy Hard-Coded into UniRule `IEA` (`PAVIR_TILLER_AXILLARY_BUD_OUTGROWTH`)**:
   - In switchgrass [`PAVIR_TILLER_AXILLARY_BUD_OUTGROWTH`](../../curation/plants/genesets/PAVIR_TILLER_AXILLARY_BUD_OUTGROWTH.yaml), 7 plasma-membrane `ABCB` auxin efflux transporters (`ABCB1`/`ABCB19` homologs regulating crown tiller bud outgrowth) fire **`GO:0043481` (`anthocyanin accumulation in tissues in response to UV light`, $7/34$ genes, $p_{\text{adj}} = 2.15 \times 10^{-18}$)** and **`GO:0090374` (`oligopeptide export from mitochondrion`, $7/34$ genes, $p_{\text{adj}} = 4.74 \times 10^{-11}$)**. UniRule (`UR000101286` / `IPR001140`) hard-codes a secondary *Arabidopsis* *abcb19* hypocotyl UV-B/flavonoid phenotype and mitochondrial `MDL`/`ATM` ABCB transport across all plant `ABCB` paralogs.

## 6. Practical Recommendations for Plant & DOE/BER Functional Genomics

1. **Choose the evidence slice according to the species' Annotation Tier**:
   - **Tier 1 (*Arabidopsis*)**: Always run `all` as the primary discovery view (`Core Recall = 0.925`, `Mechanistic Recall = 0.962`), because `iba_iea` loses more than half of plant-specific mechanistic terms (`0.442`). Use `iba_iea` (`Judged Precision = 0.929`) as a secondary specificity filter to strip out `HDA` whole-leaf proteomics artifacts (`mRNA binding` `GO:0003729`) and `IMP` organ-reuse pleiotropy.
   - **Tier 2 (*Populus*, *Sorghum*, *Brachypodium*)**: Pair `iba_iea` (`Core Recall = 0.843`) with `iba` (`Judged Precision = 0.780`, false positives cut from `24/27` to `13/27`). Any term that fires in `iba_iea` but vanishes in `iba` — such as `lignin catabolic process` (`GO:0046274`) on plant laccases, `histidine ammonia-lyase activity` (`GO:0004397`) on `PAL` genes, or `salicin transport` (`GO:0042948`) on monocot sucrose transporters — should be flagged as a likely cross-kingdom or *in vitro* `IEA` rule artifact.
   - **Tier 3 (*Panicum virgatum*, *Thlaspi arvense*)**: Never filter out `IEA` annotations (`iba` recall is `0.000`). Instead, interpret catalytic MF/BP terms alongside curated pathway gene sets (`curation/plants/genesets/lit_members.gmt`) to bridge higher-order physiological `annotation_gap`s (`C4 photosynthesis` `GO:0009760`, `seed oilbody biogenesis` `GO:0010344`).
2. **Suppress or flag GO–ChEBI chemical roll-ups and vertebrate anatomical roll-ups in plant enrichment reports**:
   - In plant datasets, `GO:0097305` (`response to alcohol`) and `GO:0033993` (`response to lipid`) are almost always structural shadows of `GO:0009737` (`response to abscisic acid`) or `GO:0009739` (`response to gibberellin`); `GO:0072351` (`tricarboxylic acid biosynthetic process`) is frequently driven by nicotianamine synthase (`GO:0030418`); and `GO:0003013` (`circulatory system process`) is an animal-centric roll-up of `GO:0010233` (`phloem transport`).
